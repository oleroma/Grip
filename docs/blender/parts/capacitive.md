# Capacitive plate

`capacitive` is a part in the middle of the ABXY cluster (object origin at X 49, Y −31, Z 40, rotated 45°), generated by the modifier group `capacitive plate` with `plate` on; its case opening is cut by the cutter `capacitive plate cutout` with `plate` off [wiring]. Its settings live in the **ABXY** preset slot, on the node `capacitive plate parameters` inside `GripV abxy` / `GripX abxy` [wiring]. I read it as a touch surface between the face buttons [inferred from the name and position].

## The three modes

The menu `capacitive` (disabled, plate, knob) is turned into two booleans inside `capacitive plate parameters`: `capacitive` (true for plate and knob) and `knob` [wiring].

| Mode | Part | Case top opening | GripV size, volume | GripX size, volume |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `knob` (GripV as saved) | small round knob | `capacitive plate cutout` (knob) | 11.4 × 11.4 × 17.14, 1,030 mm³ | 11.4 × 11.4 × 16.1, 1,074 mm³ |
| `plate` (GripX as saved) | 37.6 mm plate around the ABXY buttons | `capacitive plate cutout` (plate) | 37.6 × 37.6 × 16.04, 1,570 mm³ | 37.6 × 37.6 × 15.0, 1,678 mm³ |
| `disabled` | empty | `capacitive plate smd cutout` (fixed mesh, only clears the SMD part) | — | — |

Case top volume change against `knob`: plate −1,337 mm³ (GripV) / −1,366 (GripX); disabled +1,118 / +1,060 [measured].

## Inputs of `capacitive plate parameters`

| Input | GripV | GripX | Effect [measured] |
|---|---|---|---|
| capacitive | knob | plate | see above |
| screw hole | on | off | a hole through the part: on removes about 70–98 mm³ (knob and plate) |
| capacitive plate tolerance | 0.4 | 0.4 | plate mode only: +0.2 makes the plate 0.2 smaller in X and Y |
| capacitive plate stem tolerance | −0.1 | −0.1 | no effect in knob or plate mode |
| plate button offset | 1 | 1 | plate mode only: +0.2 makes the plate 0.4 larger and the case opening 47 mm³ larger |

Interface defaults of the group (for a new node): plate, screw hole off, tolerance 0.4, stem tolerance −0.1, offset 1 [wiring]. `capacitive plate advanced parameters` (2 inputs) exists but only the GripR1 preset uses it [wiring].

## Export

The case-top presets export every case top twice, `…_cap_plate` and `…_cap_knob`, by overriding the *Menu Switch* inside the shared `capacitive plate parameters` group, which works for both variants [wiring]. The part itself is exported by the "Parts" presets through `GripV abxy`; in the GripX preset that target is wrong, and `GripX/parts/capacitive/capacitive_knob.stl` and `capacitive_plate.stl` are byte-identical plate parts [measured] (see [Issues](../09-issues.md)).
